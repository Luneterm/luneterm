# Lunegit terminal bug ledger

Bugs and gaps found in Lunegit's terminal (as of 2026-10-29), and what Luneterm must do about each.
Paths refer to the Lunegit repo. Every fixed bug gets a regression test here too.

| # | Issue (Lunegit) | Status there | Luneterm |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wide glyphs spill onto the next cell | Fixed (glyph containment) | Fixed in `lune-termview` (M2): Lunegit's rule ported with its cases (`contain` unit tests, `fit_scale_lunegit_cases`, `allowance_cases`), plus a per-glyph clip rect in the shader and the font-independent `containment_no_spill` test (CJK, emoji, combining flood, box drawing) |
| 2 | Glyphs overflow vertically into adjacent rows | Open, by design | Fixed in `lune-termview` (M2): every glyph instance carries a clip rect whose vertical extent is its row, enforced in the fragment shader (decision D5); covered by `containment_no_spill` |

| 5 | VS16 emoji (e.g. ⚠ + U+FE0F) must be 2 cells | Fixed (custom unicode provider) | Fixed in `lune-vt` (M7 step 4): `UnicodeV11Vs16` (`ext/vs16.rs`, Unicode version `"11-vs16"`) wraps the ported Unicode 11 provider and promotes a U+FE0F that joins a width-1 character to width 2; every other result is the base provider's. | 6 | Output flood: 534–908 projections/s vs 60 fps | Fixed (16 ms throttle) | Fixed by design in `luneterm` (M9 step 3, plan D1/D2): the UI takes at most one outstanding `Update` per frame, and the engine builds the next only after the UI took the last (hidden: at most every 100 ms), so a flood costs one grid mirror per frame instead of one per read; the reader, the engine and the writer are separate threads, and output is parsed in 4 ms slices with events and damage drained after every write. Rows are marked dirty from `take_damage`, so termview rebuilds only those. Tests: `session_publishes_only_after_the_ui_took_the_last`, `session_hidden_publishes_at_most_every_100ms`, `session_threaded_publish_and_wake` (T5: at most one wake per Update, every Update taken, 10,000 cycles), `session_publish_wake_is_never_lost` (the lost-wake interleaving replayed by hand), `session_slices_end_at_4ms_plus_one_write`, `session_mirror_differential` (T1: the Update-built grid equals `viewport()` after every Update; 20,000 seeds in release), and e2e E6/E6k (`cargo test -p luneterm --test e2e`: 256 MiB plain, 16 MiB BEL and 4 MiB `ESC[6n` floods at three paces, every bound held; a key typed mid-flood is answered in about the time the debug engine takes to drain the 1 MiB inbox, 254 to 261 ms median on macOS debug). Mode 2026: row 14 |
